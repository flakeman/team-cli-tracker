# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard
We expect respectful, professional, and constructive collaboration.

Examples of expected behavior:
- be respectful in communication
- discuss ideas, not people
- provide actionable technical feedback

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- doxxing or publishing private information
- disruptive behavior in issues/PR discussions

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for violations.

## Scope
This applies to repository spaces (issues, PRs, discussions, reviews).

## Contact
For conduct concerns, contact project maintainers privately.
